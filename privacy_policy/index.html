Privacy Policy for Ultimate Haxor

Effective Date: November 26, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you play games or use software developed by Ultimate Haxor ("we," "us," or "our"). Ultimate Haxor is an independent, non-corporate development group.

We respect your privacy and do not directly collect, store, or sell your personal data on our own servers. However, to provide multiplayer functionality and cross-platform services, our software integrates with third-party platforms that process certain data on our behalf.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

Ultimate Haxor does not operate proprietary backend databases to harvest user data. We do not directly collect, track, or store:

  • Your real name, physical address, or phone number.
  • Payment or financial information.
  • Microphone audio or camera/passthrough video feeds from your VR headset.
  • Behavioral tracking or advertising profiles.

3. Third-Party Services and Data Processing

To enable online multiplayer and platform integration, our games rely on third-party infrastructure. When you play our games, data is transmitted between your device and these services:

A. Epic Online Services (EOS)

We use Epic Online Services (provided by Epic Games, Inc.) to power multiplayer features such as matchmaking, lobbies, peer-to-peer networking, and player authentication. When you connect to multiplayer sessions, EOS may automatically process:

  • Account & Device Identifiers: Epic Account IDs, device IDs, or platform-specific user IDs.
  • Network Data: Your IP address and connection telemetry (such as ping and packet loss) required to establish and maintain multiplayer connections.
  • Gameplay & Session Data: Temporary session states, lobby membership, and in-game actions necessary to synchronize gameplay between players.

Data processed through EOS is governed by the Epic Games Privacy Policy.

B. Meta Horizon Platform (VR Integration)

When playing on Meta Quest or via the Meta Horizon platform, our software interacts with Meta’s platform services and passes necessary authentication tokens to Epic Online Services to enable cross-platform multiplayer. Through this integration, the following data may be processed:

  • Meta Platform Identifiers: Your Meta Horizon User ID, Oculus ID, and public display name/username.
  • Hardware & VR Telemetry: Basic device compatibility data and spatial tracking states (such as head and hand/controller coordinates) transmitted exclusively in real time during multiplayer sessions so other players can see your avatar move. We do not record or store movement, biometric, or room-scale spatial data.

Data processed by Meta is governed by the Meta Privacy Policy.

4. How Information Is Used

The limited technical and account data processed through EOS and Meta Horizon is used strictly to:

  • Authenticate your account and verify platform entitlement.
  • Match you with other players and host multiplayer sessions.
  • Synchronize player avatars, voice chat (if applicable via EOS), and game states in real time.
  • Maintain network security and prevent cheating or abuse.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Because Ultimate Haxor does not store personal data on private servers, we do not retain your personal records. Temporary multiplayer session data is discarded automatically once a match or session ends.

For data retained by Epic Games or Meta (such as platform account logs), you can manage or request deletion of your data directly through your Epic Games and Meta account privacy settings.

6. Children’s Privacy

Our games are not directed toward children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly facilitate the collection of personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as we add new features or release on new platforms. Any updates will be reflected by changing the "Effective Date" at the top of this document.
